- [ ] **"Split personalities by default": agentic AI and the orders of mind.** Read Robert Kegan's five orders of mind (impulsive, imperial, socialized, self-authoring, self-transforming; *The Evolving Self*, *In Over Our Heads*) against Benjamin Bratton's *Agentworld* brief (Antikythera, https://cdn.antikythera.org/agentworld/Antikythera_AGENTWORLD-Brief.pdf), which argues the self will settle "less on the fortification of the boundaries of an individual and more upon their playful activation", with subjectivities that "run in functional parallel". Thesis: agents and many simultaneous selves push culture toward Order 5, while most adults sit at Order 3 or 4, so the shift looks like "psychosis" to many and settles unevenly. Threads to pull: the idea of delinking performed subjectivity from functional agency; Simondon's individuation; Michael Levin's multi-scale selves; a functionalist reading of the self as something that runs, not something that is.
  - *Notes from chapter 3, "The Mirror Talks Back" (2026-11-07).* A tempting reading is "humans hold intention, agents execute." The text resists it. 3.1 starts there: users train agents on their preferences and send out "shadow-selves speaking on their behalf", some with "legal standing to execute contracts". But 3.2 rejects "hierarchical domestication": users will "come to resemble their agents as much as the agents will resemble their users", and how much of the agent is you "will be a matter of ongoing discovery and negotiation". 3.3 is where the title comes from: "Personalities become split personalities by default." Chapter 2 undercuts the premise further: if agents delink performed subjectivity from functional agency (2.3) and there is "no homunculus waiting in the weights" (2.4), then the human side may be laminated too, and intention is something the composite (2.6) produces rather than something a human holds and hands off. 1.2 floats humans moving to "value-setting, oversight, and long-horizon judgment", then asks "Or, something else?" Essay angle: the intention/execution split is an Order 4 picture (self-authoring boss, obedient tools); Bratton's composite, where intention is co-produced, is closer to Order 5.
