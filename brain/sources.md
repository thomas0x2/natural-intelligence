A starting list of places to watch. Add to it whenever something proves useful, and remove things that go stale. Each line: name, link, one line on why it is worth watching.

## Ideas and long-form

- Antikythera · https://antikythera.org · Berggruen Institute program on the philosophy of planetary-scale computation. Its journal (https://journal.antikythera.org) and MIT Press book series are core reading for this catalog. Publisher of *What Is Intelligence?*.
- Noema Magazine · https://www.noemamag.com · Berggruen Institute's magazine; frequent long essays on AI, minds and technology.
- Quanta Magazine · https://www.quantamagazine.org · The best general-audience science journalism on neuroscience, computation and math.
- The Transmitter · https://www.thetransmitter.org · Neuroscience news and essays from the Simons Foundation.
- Asterisk Magazine · https://asteriskmag.com · Careful long-form essays on AI and science.
- The Gradient · https://thegradient.pub · Essays and interviews on AI research, written by researchers.

## Research and papers

- arXiv (cs.LG, cs.AI, q-bio.NC, cs.AR) · https://arxiv.org · The firehose. Use listings and filters, not the front page.
- bioRxiv neuroscience · https://www.biorxiv.org/collection/neuroscience · Preprints from the brain side.
- Transformer Circuits Thread · https://transformer-circuits.pub · Mechanistic interpretability work with unusually good diagrams.
- Distill (archive) · https://distill.pub · No longer publishing, but the standard for explanatory visual writing.

## Open models, code and data

- Hugging Face · https://huggingface.co · Open weights, datasets and the people behind them.
- EleutherAI · https://www.eleuther.ai · Open research collective, open models and evaluation tools.
- Allen Institute for AI · https://allenai.org · Fully open models with open training data and code.
- Papers with Code successors and GitHub trending (ML) · Track which papers ship working code.

## Neuroscience

- Allen Institute for Brain Science · https://alleninstitute.org · Open brain atlases and cell type data.
- OpenWorm · https://openworm.org · Open project to simulate *C. elegans*, a functionalist touchstone.
- FlyWire · https://flywire.ai · The whole-brain connectome of the fruit fly, openly available.
- Neuromatch Academy · https://neuromatch.io · Free courses in computational neuroscience and deep learning.
- BrainGlobe · https://brainglobe.info · Open-source tools for brain atlases and image analysis.
- Thousand Brains Project · https://thousandbrains.org · Open-source sensorimotor learning based on cortical theory.

## Books

- *What Is Intelligence?* by Blaise Agüera y Arcas (MIT Press, 2025) · https://whatisintelligence.antikythera.org · Functionalist case that life is computation and intelligence is prediction. Free online. Featured in Edition 000.

## Agents and AI tooling

- Paperclip · https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip · Open-source (MIT) orchestration for teams of AI agents run like a company. Featured in Edition 000.

## Hardware and silicon

- Tiny Tapeout · https://tinytapeout.com · Get your own chip design fabricated cheaply.
- OpenROAD · https://theopenroadproject.org · Open-source RTL-to-GDS chip design flow.
- SkyWater and GlobalFoundries open PDKs · https://github.com/google/skywater-pdk · Open process design kits.
- RISC-V International · https://riscv.org · The open instruction set architecture.
- CHIPS Alliance · https://www.chipsalliance.org · Open-source hardware tools and IP.

## Commons and policy

- Open Source Initiative · https://opensource.org · Including the Open Source AI Definition.
- Software Freedom Conservancy · https://sfconservancy.org · Licensing and enforcement.

## People and newsletters

Add individuals here as we find voices worth following. Note what each is good for.
