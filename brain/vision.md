> We are as minds, and might as well get good at it.

## Purpose

The Natural Intelligence Catalog is a field guide to the tools and ideas of the late 2020s for understanding, building and living alongside minds, both the ones that grew in skulls and the ones we are now growing in silicon.

The Whole Earth Catalog gave its readers *access to tools* so that individuals could shape their own environment. The environment has changed. The most consequential tools now think, or appear to, and they are increasingly built behind closed doors. This catalog exists to keep access open: to point readers at the models, papers, datasets, instruments, chips and people that let an individual or a small group understand intelligence and build with it on their own terms.

## Who it is for

- The independent builder who wants to train, run, probe or fabricate something without permission from a platform.
- The researcher who reads across the line between neuroscience and machine learning and finds most venues stay on one side of it.
- The curious generalist who wants a map of the territory that is honest about what is known, what is hype and what is still open.

## What it is

Each edition is a curated, visual issue of the catalog. It is published on the web as the permanent archive and sent by email to readers. An edition mixes three kinds of material:

1. **Access to tools.** Short, opinionated reviews of things you can actually use: open models, libraries, datasets, dev boards, open PDKs, lab protocols, books.
2. **Ideas.** Essays, excerpts and diagrams on how minds work, in brains and in machines, and what follows from that.
3. **Art.** Visual work that helps readers see the subject: generative pieces, scientific imagery, diagrams, schematics, photography.

## What it is not

- Not a news feed. If it will be irrelevant in a month it probably does not belong.
- Not a product launch channel. Nothing is listed because someone paid for it.
- Not a hype or a doom outlet. The tone is practical and curious.

## The test for every item

Would a capable, curious person be meaningfully more able to understand or build something after following this link? If not, cut it.
